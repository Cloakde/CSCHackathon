## Controls inspected and exercised

- `transport.ts` and its tests: fixed endpoint/header auth; redirect refusal; bounded UTF-8 request/response and body-read cancellation; one reservation before dispatch; no automatic transport retry; late fetch/body rejection cleanup; literal and decoded credential-echo rejection; safe errors; unknown usage charged fully.
- `index.ts`, `schemas.ts`, `prompts.ts` and tests: generation and verification remain separate; identities/citations are checked against supplied evidence; verifiers receive selected evidence, not unrelated passages; oracle answers are not imported into provider prompts; unsupported verdicts remain rejections.
- `budget.ts` and tests: durable reservation before sending, single active request, upward rounding, $1/32-attempt ceiling, crash/restart recovery, lock ownership, policy/source mismatch, interrupted charges and sealed completion. The correction changes the policy hash, not the plan ID or ledger location. Existing incompatible ledgers must remain intact and stop; they must never be reset for a new allowance.
- Launcher/report/scenario tests: default command only prints a plan; execution requires a separately approved clean tree; ordinary app/tests remain prewritten/injected; reports preserve interruption and final accounting. Frozen cases and public wire contracts are unchanged.
